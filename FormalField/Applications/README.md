# FormalField Applications Layer

This layer sits above the frozen FormalField Core L1–L15.
It does not modify the core.
It registers applications as structured obligations.

## Applications Included

- `RHApplication`
- `TOEApplication`
- `RSAApplication`
- `PhysicsApplication`

## RHApplication

- Structural obligations: `xiCarrier`, `metricReadout`, `canonicalChannel`, `noSecondAmplitudeGate`, `primePositiveTransport`, `classicalZeroCorrespondence`.
- Current status: Pending external bridge.
- Required to mark it closed: prove the prime-positive transport bridge and classical zero correspondence in addition to the core-ready obligations.
- Not claimed: no proof of RH, no theorem about all zeros on the critical line, and no analytic closure claim.

## TOEApplication

- Structural obligations: `stateSpace`, `localConsistency`, `symmetryDescent`, `observableReconstruction`, `causalDescent`, `globalCompatibility`.
- Current status: Reduced, not globally proven.
- Required to mark it closed: supply the global causal and compatibility obligations on top of the structural representation obligations.
- Not claimed: no complete physical theory, no empirical finality, and no TOE theorem.

## RSAApplication

- Structural obligations: `logCarrier`, `reciprocalManifold`, `orthogonalProjection`, `thetaMellinFilter`, `localLyapunovClosure`, `basinEntryComplexity`, `hardVerification`.
- Current status: Conditional local closure, global complexity open.
- Required to mark it closed: resolve the open basin-entry complexity obligation together with the local and verification obligations.
- Not claimed: no RSA break, no attack code, and no complexity collapse claim.

## PhysicsApplication

- Structural obligations: `carrier`, `metric`, `dimension`, `field`, `passage`, `flow`, `effect`, `readout`, `empiricalCalibration`.
- Current status: Structural mapping only, empirical calibration pending.
- Required to mark it closed: supply empirical calibration and independent measurement on top of the structural mapping obligations.
- Not claimed: no physical prediction, no TOE claim, and no empirical closure claim.
